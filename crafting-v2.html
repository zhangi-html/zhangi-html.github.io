Crafting XP v2

Testing experience & catch-up mechanics. Type a gold amount to preview it, hover any multiplier to see how it's built.

Proficiency in one trade accelerates XP gain in any trade that shares its disciplines and aptitudes — each qualifying trade adds +1 to the multiplier applied to XP gained. Sharing 1 skill helps until you're within 2 of its bonus, sharing 2 helps until you're within 1, and sharing 3 helps all the way until you match it.

Earning XP: Major trades earn XP equal to the value of the item crafted. Gathering trades earn XP equal to the value of the material gathered. Minor trades earn XP faster than majors for comparable work (exact rate still TBD).
